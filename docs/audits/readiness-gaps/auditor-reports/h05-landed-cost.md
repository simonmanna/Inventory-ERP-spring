# Auditor report: landed cost (read-only audit of main 46281ee2, 2026-11-17)

Line numbers refer to 46281ee2. Findings fixed since then are listed in [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md).

VERDICT (landed cost): Ready after fixes — the voucher engine is sound (atomic posting, exact allocation, lineage that ties to the GL), but the supplier-invoice controls, approval controls, credit vouchers and the legacy path that is still in use can double-count or strand cost.

FINDINGS (ranked, most severe first, max 15):
Paths: J = backend/src/main/java/com/inventoryerp, M = backend/src/main/resources/db/migration.

F1 [P1] Supplier bills generated by a voucher skip every vendor-bill duplicate check
  where: J/invoicing/application/VendorBillService.java:253-272 (the guard loop runs only in the ordinary post, :403-405); J/procurement/application/LandedCostVoucherService.java:1355-1365; J/procurement/application/BillControls.java:38-67; J/procurement/application/ProcurementPolicyRegistry.java:95-98
  what: The duplicate registry compares an invoice only with other vouchers. A generated bill never reaches BillControls, and that check is off by default anyway.
  scenario: AP posts freight invoice FR-77 (5,000) as an ordinary bill. Costing then enters FR-77 on a voucher with billMode=generate. Result: a second posted bill, AP of 10,000 for one invoice, the cost booked twice, and both bills payable.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none; known: P1-9 (partly fixed)

F2 [P1] The GRN screen still uses the legacy engine
  where: web/src/features/procurement/landed-cost-panel.tsx:56-81; J/procurement/application/LandedCostLayers.java:661-664; J/procurement/application/LandedCostService.java:178-190, 270
  what: This matches Nest (docs/spec/procurement.md:87; owner approval still pending), but it is a real production risk:
    - `onHand = min(pool, receipt qty)` puts the share of already-sold units into stock once other receipts have refilled the pool;
    - there are no approval, duplicate or tax checks;
    - goods-receipt permissions are enough to credit any postable account.
  scenario: AVCO item, receipt of 100, 60 sold, pool refilled to 200, charge 1,000 → all 1,000 goes to stock (correct split: 400 stock / 600 COGS). Or the credit account is a bank account → Dr stock / Cr bank.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none; known: implementation-map F5 (still open on this path)

F3 [P1] Linked supplier bills are not validated and can be linked repeatedly
  where: LandedCostVoucherService.java:1214-1234, 1342; M/V14__landed_cost_supplier_bills.sql:16 (the index is not unique)
  what: The link checks only that the bill exists, is not cancelled and has the same supplier. It does not check that the bill debits 2155, its amount or currency, or what is already linked to it. Linked charges also skip the duplicate registry.
  scenario: bill B (5,000) is booked to freight expense, then linked on voucher V1 → 5,000 in expense plus 5,000 in stock. Or B is linked on both V1 and V2 → stock up 10,000. Only the LC-3 2155 row shows the gap, and only afterwards.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none (multiSupplierShipment covers only the normal case); known: new

F4 [P1] No segregation of duties on voucher approval or on duplicate overrides
  where: LandedCostVoucherService.java:415-437, 1417-1436; J/kernel/approvals/SegregationOfDutiesService.java:52-64; VendorBillService.java:247-252
  what: Approval never compares the approver with createdBy or submittedBy, and no SoD pair covers vouchers. A duplicate override is approved by the person who requests it. A generated bill also skips bill approval and the supplier_bill_approval SoD pair.
  scenario: one user with create, approve and post rights (a normal accountant role) creates a voucher with a generate charge to any supplier, approves it and posts it → a posted payable that no second person reviewed.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none; known: new

F5 [P1] A draft credit voucher survives the reversal of its original and can still be posted
  where: LandedCostVoucherService.java:554-559, 474-545, 835-841
  what: Reversal blocks only credit vouchers that are submitted, approved or posted. Submit, approve and post never check that the original voucher is still posted.
  scenario: V1 posts 10,000 → credit C1 (2,000) drafted → V1 reversed → C1 approved and posted → Cr stock 2,000 / Dr 2155 2,000 for cost that was already removed. Stock ends 2,000 below cost and 2155 is in debit; the supplier credit note then puts AP into debit.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none; known: new

F6 [P1] A legacy landed-cost post can still race a GRN reversal
  where: LandedCostService.java:201-217; J/procurement/application/GoodsReceiptsService.java:460-472
  what: The legacy post moves the cost from draft to posted and reads the GRN without locking it. Meanwhile:
    1. the reversal's re-check inside its transaction still reads the committed 'draft';
    2. its UPDATE that cancels drafts waits on the row lock, then skips the row, which is now posted;
    3. the reversal commits.
  scenario: user A posts LC-1 on GRN-9 while user B reverses GRN-9 → both succeed, leaving capitalised freight on goods that were reversed. Vouchers are protected because 'approved' is counted, unless both approve and post happen inside the reversal's transaction.
  confidence: likely (interleaving traced in code, not run); test covering it: none (shipmentFifoAndIsolation tests only the sequential case); known: P1-6

F7 [P2] Reversing a STANDARD voucher sends released adjunct to COGS even when it went to WIP or scrap
  where: LandedCostLayers.java:415-421, 444-447; J/inventory/application/CostLineageService.java:439-459
  what: The release rows record where each amount actually went, but the reversal ignores them. The cascade guard (:390-398) triggers only after a cost run has posted.
  scenario: 1,000 in 1396 on 100 units; 30 units issued to an open production order (300 released to WIP); voucher reversed → Cr COGS 300 instead of Cr WIP 300. WIP is overstated and COGS understated by 300.
  confidence: likely; test covering it: none (reverseAfterProductionConsumption covers AVCO only); known: new

F8 [P2] The LC-3 reconciliation shows a false 2156 difference after a partial clear
  where: J/procurement/application/LandedCostReconciliationService.java:99-104
  what: It uses status='posted' and `amount`. It should use `remainingAmount` over 'posted' and 'partially_cleared', as J/costing/application/LandedCostAccrualService.java:801-806 does and docs/adr/ADR-S053-landed-cost-erp-grade.md:226 specifies.
  scenario: a 1,000 accrual cleared by 400 → GL shows 600, expected shows 0 → clean=false at every close, which hides real differences.
  confidence: confirmed; test covering it: none; known: new

F9 [P2] ACR-14: accrual clearing after an assigned lineage exception
  where: J/inventory/application/LineageExceptionService.java:130-148; J/inventory/application/LineageFollower.java:325; LandedCostAccrualService.java:546-557, 570-584, 657-680
  what: Assigning an exception writes no release row and records a new adjustment with no parent. The clearing then:
    - credits the parked child adjustment to COGS;
    - debits COGS for the same amount as "uncovered";
    - credits the new adjustment's on-hand share to COGS.
    For STANDARD items this nets out. For AVCO items, the on-hand share comes out of COGS instead of stock.
  scenario: it needs all of these: an accrual, a parked share (unpaired transfer, cycle, or depth over 20), assignment rather than write-off, an AVCO item, and goods still on hand at clearing. The error is at most that on-hand share and reverses as those units sell. Likelihood is low.
  confidence: likely; test covering it: none; known: ACR-14

F10 [P2] Receipt units are matched to GRN lines by product only, in millisecond-timestamp order
  where: LandedCostVoucherService.java:1036, 1059-1083, 935, 985; GoodsReceiptsService.java:348; J/inventory/application/StockService.java:1208
  what: Receipt stock rows are written in variant-id order and matched to the first unused GRN line of the same product, ignoring the variant. Rows with the same timestamp also make the unit that takes the rounding residue depend on the query plan.
  scenario: line 1 is shirt/L, line 2 is shirt/M, and M's variant id sorts first → a manual 300/700 split, or appliesToLineIds=[line 1], lands on the wrong size.
  confidence: likely (the line mismatch); suspected (order flips — confirm with rows in the same millisecond); test covering it: none; known: new

F13 [P2] The lineage split is computed before the stock lock is taken

STRENGTHS:
- Concurrency and approval checks on posting: posting claims the voucher atomically, reverse and credit lock the voucher row, and posting re-checks the approved hash and a per-target tolerance (concurrentPosts, hashRefusesChangedInputs, largeMovementNeedsReapproval).
- Allocation is exact: one shared calculator puts the rounding residue on the last unit at 6 dp, and the sum is asserted (LandedCostVoucherService.java:1003-1008). Zero basis, zero quantity and missing weight or volume are refused before any division.
- Lineage shares add up to the charge and each maps to one account: stock, COGS, WIP, 1396 or 5976. Reversal is refused once a share was cascaded or resolved.
- The voucher-to-voucher duplicate registry normalises invoice references, uses an advisory lock and a partial unique index, and allows single-use, audited overrides (concurrentDuplicateInvoice, normalisedDuplicates).
- Customs and tax:
  - customs tariffs are versioned by effective date;
  - calculations are made immutable by a trigger (M/V295__lc7_customs_tariffs_templates.sql:78-92);
  - manual customs adjustments need a permission and a reason, and are audited;
  - each invoice must use one currency and one rate, and realised FX is posted on payment;
  - unreviewed tax is refused (unreviewedTaxGuard).

- A multi-variant GRN with manual or line-scoped charges; a lot spanning two receipts; a STANDARD reversal after a release into WIP.
